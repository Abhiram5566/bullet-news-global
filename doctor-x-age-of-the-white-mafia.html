THE STORY

About the Drama

A medical noir about gifted surgeon Gye Soo Jung, who challenges a hospital establishment shaped by corruption and insists that skill and patient care should come first.

Native Title: 닥터 X: 하얀 마피아의 시대

THE HOOK

✨ Why Fans Are Excited

  • The medical-noir format turns the hospital into a battleground over power, ethics and corruption.
  • Gye Su-jeong is presented as a gifted surgeon willing to challenge a system she believes is broken.
  • The story brings medical dilemmas into conflict with institutional politics.

BNG EDITORIAL

📰 Bullet News Review

Doctor X moves beyond routine hospital cases and into a fight with the institution itself. Its central appeal is a surgeon whose talent comes with an uncompromising attitude, placing patient care and hospital politics in direct conflict. The noir approach should work best when systemic stakes stay connected to individual patients.

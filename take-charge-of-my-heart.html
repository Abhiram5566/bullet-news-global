BNG EDITORIAL

📰 Bullet News Review

Take Charge of My Heart stands out with a high-concept meet-cute: one lead needs an artificial-heart recharge, while the other has an unusual electrical ability. That offers a clear comic engine, but the emotional payoff will depend on how the story develops their vulnerabilities beyond the fantasy gimmick. The premise is distinctive and easy to remember.

Our Editorial Approach

Bullet News Global aims to present information in a concise, understandable and transparent format.

Verification

We aim to verify factual claims using reliable sources and, where appropriate, primary or official sources.

Attribution

Information originating from external sources is attributed to those sources. We distinguish reported information from analysis, commentary and editorial material.

Corrections

If a published article contains a factual error, we aim to correct the information and update the relevant article when appropriate.

Transparency

Bullet News Global seeks to distinguish between independently verified information, information reported by other sources and information that remains unconfirmed.

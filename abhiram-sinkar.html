About Abhiram Sinkar

Abhiram Sinkar is a founder, developer and digital creator with a strong interest in technology, artificial intelligence and digital media. As CEO of Rudra Works and Founder & Publisher of Bullet News Global (BNG), he is dedicated to building digital platforms, exploring new ideas and bringing technology and information together in meaningful ways.

Also known as Rudra Sinkar, Abhiram has developed his work across web development, AI-related tools and digital publishing. Through Bullet News Global, he has published numerous articles covering a range of topics and established an internship initiative designed to introduce aspiring writers and students to journalism, encourage their writing skills and help them gain practical experience in researching and presenting information.

A hardworking and curious individual, Abhiram believes in continuous learning, taking on new challenges and developing his skills through practical experience. Beyond technology and publishing, he has a keen interest in exploring different cultures, learning languages and understanding perspectives from around the world. His ambition is to keep learning, create useful digital projects and build platforms that connect people with knowledge, ideas and opportunities.

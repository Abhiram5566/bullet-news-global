About Abhiram Sinkar

Abhiram Sinkar is a digital creator and developer whose work spans web development, AI-related tools and digital media. He is the CEO of Rudra Works and the founder and publisher of Bullet News Global (BNG), a digital news and information platform covering global affairs, India, China, Korea, technology, esports and entertainment.

Through Rudra Works, Abhiram develops digital projects and explores practical uses of technology. His work on BNG includes shaping the publication's direction, maintaining its web infrastructure, improving publishing and content-management workflows, and helping make the website's information accessible to readers.

BNG publishes news updates, explainers, longer articles and entertainment coverage. As founder and publisher, Abhiram oversees the platform's overall direction; individual stories should be evaluated by their displayed author bylines and the sources included with each article.

He is interested in building useful digital products and combining software development with creative publishing. His public project work includes the development and maintenance of BNG's website and publishing systems. This profile connects his work with Bullet News Global and Rudra Works and will be updated as additional public projects and confirmed bylined contributions are documented.

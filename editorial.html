How our editorial review works

Reader submissions are leads for review, not published articles. Editors check context, dates, source reliability, attribution and whether claims can be independently corroborated. Material may be declined or returned for clarification.

Publication controls

  • Submitting a story never publishes it directly.
  • Only authorised CMS editors can prepare and approve content.
  • Unverified allegations should not be presented as established fact.
  • Corrections should be handled transparently.

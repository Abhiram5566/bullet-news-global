📰 Bullet News Review

Shadow Punisher has a strong mystery hook: a detective unit investigates strange cases that point toward a larger threat. The story will be most compelling when each case develops the characters and advances the wider conspiracy. Its historical-fantasy atmosphere and ensemble give it a clear identity among costume dramas.

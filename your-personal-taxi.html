THE STORY

About the Drama

A healing music drama about taxi driver Go Myung Seok, whose unusual cab carries passengers with everyday lives and extraordinary stories. Each journey is accompanied by a song chosen by the passenger.

Native Title: 개인적인 택시

Country: South Korea 🇰🇷

Episodes: 8

Status: Airing

Genres: Healing • Music • Human Drama

Main Cast: Cha Tae Hyun, Im Se Mi, Joo Jong Hyuk

THE HOOK

✨ Why Fans Are Excited

  • The taxi format lets each passenger's story and meaningful song become a small emotional portrait.
  • A recurring driver creates a steady centre for stories about very different lives.
  • The healing tone offers an alternative to plot-heavy thrillers.

BNG EDITORIAL

📰 Bullet News Review

Your Personal Taxi has an appealing simple device: passengers share parts of their lives through a ride and songs tied to their memories. That structure can make the show feel like a collection of small emotional portraits, while the recurring driver gives the season a steady centre. It is a good fit for viewers who enjoy reflective character stories.

An editorial preview from Bullet News Global—not a viewer rating or a claim of consensus among fans.

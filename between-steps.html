THE STORY

About the Drama

This romance follows former swimmer Park Min Jae, who lost his right leg due to illness, and graduate student Im Yoo Jin, who is searching for a new direction in life.

THE HOOK

✨ Why Fans Are Excited

  • The university robotics-lab setting gives the romance a different backdrop from a typical office or school series.
  • Both leads are rebuilding their plans after life changes, giving their connection an emotional foundation.
  • The quieter premise leaves room for naturalistic character growth alongside the romance.

BNG EDITORIAL

📰 Bullet News Review

Between Steps centres on two people whose plans have changed and who meet while working in a robotics lab. The specialised setting and slow-building relationship could keep it distinctive, while the characters' uncertainty gives the romance emotional stakes. Its success will depend on making their individual growth as engaging as the relationship itself.

📰 Bullet News Review

Between Steps centres on two people whose plans have changed and who meet while working in a robotics lab. The specialised setting and slow-building relationship could keep it distinctive, while the characters' uncertainty gives the romance emotional stakes. Its success will depend on making their individual growth as engaging as the relationship itself.

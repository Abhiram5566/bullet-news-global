Allegations of Unofficial Payments for Government Hostel Admissions Raise Public Concern

By Bullet News Investigations Team
July 2026 • Hyderabad

Representative Image: Government residential hostel building.

📌 Quick Summary

  • Bullet News has received reports from parents and students regarding alleged unofficial payments for hostel admissions.
  • Some reports mention amounts ranging between ₹20,000 and ₹30,000.
  • Some information received by our newsroom refers to government residential hostels, including reports mentioning TGMREIS institutions and other residential education systems.
  • Bullet News has not independently verified these allegations.
  • Authorities are encouraged to investigate any credible complaints.

Reports Raise Questions

Bullet News has received multiple reports from parents, students and members of the public alleging that certain individuals are demanding unofficial payments while claiming they can secure admissions into government residential hostels. According to information shared with our newsroom, these individuals allegedly approach parents claiming they have influence over the admission process. Families are reportedly told that once admission is secured, they will not have to worry about education, food and accommodation for the next several years because government residential institutions provide these facilities. At present, Bullet News has not independently verified these allegations. These reports should not be interpreted as proof of wrongdoing by any institution or individual.

Reports From Different Institutions

Some reports received by Bullet News refer to concerns involving certain government residential hostels, including references to TGMREIS institutions and other residential education systems. Our newsroom continues collecting information and verifying claims before reaching any conclusions.

Why This Matters

Government residential hostels were established to provide quality education and equal opportunities to deserving students, especially those from economically weaker backgrounds. If unofficial payments are being demanded by anyone claiming influence over admissions, such practices would undermine fairness, transparency and merit. At the same time, if these allegations are inaccurate or involve only isolated individuals, a transparent inquiry can help protect the reputation of honest institutions and officials.

Call For Transparency

Bullet News respectfully urges the concerned education authorities to examine any credible complaints related to hostel admissions through a fair and transparent process. Parents are encouraged to:

  • Follow only official admission procedures.
  • Avoid making unofficial payments to anyone claiming they can guarantee admissions.
  • Preserve receipts, messages or documents if they believe they have been approached improperly.
  • Report suspected misconduct through the appropriate government channels.

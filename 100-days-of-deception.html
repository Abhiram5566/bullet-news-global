THE STORY

About the Drama

A period spy romance following skilled pickpocket Lee Ga Kyeong, who takes on an undercover mission for the independence movement and becomes entangled with interpreter Kim Tae Woong.

Native Title: 100일의 거짓말

Country: South Korea 🇰🇷

Status: Airing

Genres: Historical • Spy • Romance

Main Cast: Kim Yoo Jung, Park Jin Young, Kim Hyun Joo, Lee Moo Saeng, Jin Sun Kyu

THE HOOK

✨ Why Fans Are Excited

  • The historical spy premise blends romance with an undercover mission inside the colonial government.
  • The lead's resourcefulness and language skills give her a reason to enter a dangerous assignment.
  • Personal relationships sit alongside a wider struggle for Korean independence.

BNG EDITORIAL

📰 Bullet News Review

100 Days of Deception combines a historical setting with espionage and a complicated romance. The undercover mission creates a natural source of suspense, while shifting identities put trust under pressure. Its strongest moments should come when the political stakes and the characters' personal loyalties collide.

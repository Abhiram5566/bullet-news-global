THE STORY

About the Drama

A village beauty and a struggling actor work with their neighbours to protect a coastal community from a redevelopment plan and find new ways to revive the local economy.

BNG EDITORIAL

📰 Bullet News Review

King of Comedy is built around a community problem and an ensemble, giving the comedy a clear goal rather than relying only on punchlines. Its appeal will depend on how naturally the characters' rivalries and friendships grow out of that situation. Viewers looking for a lighter series may enjoy the mix of local-life details and broad comedy.

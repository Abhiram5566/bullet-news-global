📰 Bullet News Review

Legend of the Female General follows He Yan, a talented young woman who disguises herself and rises through the military ranks to protect her family and country. Adapted from the novel Rebirth of a Star General, the drama combines romance, warfare, political intrigue and personal sacrifice into an epic historical story. The strong performances of Zhou Ye and Cheng Lei helped make the series one of the most talked-about costume dramas of 2025.
